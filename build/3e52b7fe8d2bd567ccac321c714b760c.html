    summary :
    This climate data record of sea ice concentration is obtained from medium resolution passive microwave satellite data over the polar regions (AMSR-E and AMSR2). The processing chain features: 1) dynamic tuning of tie-points and algorithms, 2) correction of atmospheric noise using a Radiative Transfer Model, 3) computation of per-pixel uncertainties, and 4) an optimal hybrid sea ice concentration algorithm. This product was generated in the context of the ESA Climate Change Initiative Programme (ESA CCI). The EUMETSAT OSI SAF contributed with access and re-use of part of its processing software and facilities.
